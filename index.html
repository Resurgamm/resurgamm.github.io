Hi, I'm Boyi Li. I am pursuing a dual degree in Computer Engineering at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and Zhejiang University.

I am grateful to them for their help and support in my early research journey and development.

Research Interests

My research explores Child-Centric AI through two interconnected themes.

  1. Cognition-Inspired AI

    Study how children acquire perception, knowledge, and reasoning from limited observations and abstractions, and progressively learn about the world. Build models in the similar way.

  2. Pediatric Foundation Models

    Develop foundation models for child development and neurodevelopmental disorders to better understand children's growth trajectories, motor patterns, and social behaviors.

Future Plans

I am seeking a PhD position starting in Fall 2027, with a focus on cognition-inspired multimodal learning. If my background aligns with your research, please get in touch. I would be grateful for the chance to discuss research fit and potential collaborations.
