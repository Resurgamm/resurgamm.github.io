Research Interests

My research interest is what I call Child-Centric AI, which sits at the intersection of Multimodal LLMs, AI for healthcare, and World Models. My research explores Child-Centric AI through two interconnected themes.

  1. Cognition-Inspired AI

    Study how children acquire perception, knowledge, and reasoning from limited observations and abstractions, and progressively learn about the world. Draw inspiration from how children learning to enhance the ability of MLLMs to understand and reason about spatial relationships, 3D scenes, and temporal dynamics.

  2. Pediatric Foundation Models

    Develop foundation models for children's neurodevelopmental disorders (e.g., autism, cerebral palsy) to better understand their growth trajectories, motor patterns, and social behaviors. Design video models to find digital biomarkers to help pediatricians and parents with early screening.
